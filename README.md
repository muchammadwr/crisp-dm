### Overview of the Business Understanding Phase

The **Business Understanding** phase focuses on uncovering what the organization expects to gain from the data mining project. Taking time to clarify problems, goals, and available resources before expending valuable effort helps ensure that all stakeholders are aligned.

-----

### Key Sub-Phases & Examples

#### 1\. Determining Business Objectives

This sub-phase focuses on gaining deep insight into the business goals, clarifying the primary problem to solve, and establishing success criteria from a business perspective.

  * **Key Tasks:**
      * **Compile Business Background:** Identify organizational structures, key individuals, internal sponsors, affected business units, current solutions, and problem areas.
      * **Define Business Objectives:** Document specific, realistic problems to solve and expected business benefits (e.g., reducing churn).
      * **Establish Business Success Criteria:** Define objective measures (e.g., specific target percentages) and subjective criteria (e.g., qualitative discoveries).
  * **E-Retail Case Study Example:**
      * **Context & Objective:** An established electronics e-retailer faced stiff competition from newer web stores. To remain profitable despite rising customer acquisition costs, the company commissioned a study to cultivate existing relationships by improving cross-sales with better recommendations and increasing customer loyalty.
      * **Business Success Criteria:** Increasing cross-sales by 10%, having customers spend more time and see more pages per visit, and completing the project on time and under budget.

-----

#### 2\. Assessing the Situation

This sub-phase involves taking an inventory of current capabilities, identifying liabilities, and anticipating potential obstacles.

  * **Key Tasks:**
      * **Resource Inventory:** Audit available hardware, data sources (formats, storage, live access), and personnel (domain experts, database administrators).
      * **Requirements, Assumptions, and Constraints:** Verify security/legal requirements, project scheduling, financial budgets, and data access permissions.
      * **Risks and Contingencies:** Identify potential scheduling, financial, data, or result risks, and formulate backup plans.
      * **Terminology & Cost/Benefit Analysis:** Compile a shared glossary to align business and technical teams, and compare overall costs against potential returns.
  * **E-Retail Case Study Example:**
      * **Personnel:** The company had in-house expertise with server logs and purchase databases but lacked data warehousing and cleaning experience, prompting them to hire a data mining specialist.
      * **Data:** Plentiful web logs and purchase data were available; analysis was restricted to registered customers for the initial study.
      * **Risks & Contingencies:** To manage financial and timeline risks, the project was restricted to a single quarter, with a contingency plan to reduce the scope if time or budget limits were threatened.

-----

#### 3\. Determining Data Mining Goals

Here, abstract business goals are translated into concrete technical data mining objectives and benchmarks.

  * **Key Tasks:**
      * **Define Technical Objectives:** Specify the data mining problem types (e.g., classification, prediction, clustering) and attach clear units of time or numbers.
      * **Define Data Mining Success Criteria:** Establish technical benchmarks for model performance, accuracy, and validity.
  * **E-Retail Case Study Example:**
      * **Market Basket Analysis:** Use historical purchase data to build a model linking "related" items to display during browsing.
      * **Profiling:** Analyze web logs to determine customer intent and dynamically highlight relevant items on personalized main pages.
      * **Sequence Analysis:** Predict a user's next destination on the site based on their previous browsing path.

-----

#### 4\. Producing a Project Plan

The final sub-phase generates a comprehensive master document outlining goals, schedules, resources, risks, and decision points for all project phases.

  * **Key Tasks:**
      * Estimate required time for each phase.
      * Plan for deployment effort and identify review checkpoints.
      * Select appropriate modeling tools and techniques.
  * **E-Retail Case Study Example (Sample Project Plan):**

| Phase                      | Estimated Time | Key Resources                            | Primary Risks                    |
